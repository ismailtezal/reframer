# Showreel playbook

In the 2026 one-prompt wave, showreels took about 42% of all likes. Most of the top ones:
- ran exactly 15.1s;
- used a dark background;
- had music only;
- cut on the beat, with shape morphs and kinetic type.

They also became a cliché: hundreds of reels made from the same one-line brief share the same palette, the same grid of squares and the same closing logo. A reel only stands out if it has a concept.

## Before building
- **Concept:** pick one (time, gravity, paper, signal, growth, the brand's own shape). Then pick a signature motif (one shape, one line or one grid) that every scene transforms. Record the concept and motif in the storyboard brief.
- **Palette and type:** background #0A0A0A–#121212, foreground #EDEDED, one or two accents from the brand. One display face (heavy, condensed or geometric), plus an optional mono.
- **Track first:** get the music, read its beats, and build everything on that beat grid.

## Format
16:9 1920×1080 (or 1:1 1080 for a social loop), 30fps, exactly 15s (450f) or 20–30s. Music-only; 120–128 BPM is ideal.

## Beat math (30fps)
Frames per beat = 1800 ÷ BPM. At 120 BPM a beat is 15f (0.5s) and a bar is 60f (2s). Put a beat marker on every downbeat.

## Timeline (15s at 120 BPM)
| Time | Bars | Build |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1s | intro | Cold open with motion on frame 0: the motif shape scales in with heavy easing, or a word scrambles into place. |
| 1–5s | 1–2 | 4–6 micro-scenes, one per 1–2 beats: a kinetic-title with slam or mask-up, shape morphs, a counter, a card flip, a typographic grid. |
| 5–11s | 3–5 | Escalate: cut on every beat, use split-screen and repetition patterns, invert once (the background flips to the accent on a downbeat), and let one 300px+ word bleed off frame. |
| 11–14s | 6–7 | Resolve: everything collapses into the motif, which becomes the logo or name (logo-reveal or kinetic-title, heavy easing). |
| 14–15s | end | Hold. The last frame matches the first so the reel loops. |

## Motion vocabulary
- **Transform, don't replace:** one element changes size, corner radius, color and position into the next idea. Match cuts on shape, color or direction of travel.
- **Kinetic type:** char stagger 1–2f; slam or stretch on accented words; mask-up for calm lines; extreme scale contrast between huge and tiny type.
- **Springs:** snappy for UI-like pieces, heavy for type and impacts; overshoot ≤5%. Most motion happens in the first 8–12 frames of each beat, followed by a tiny settle.
- **Transitions:** mostly hard cuts on the beat; a whip with zoom-blur on 1–2 big moments; a flash once at most.
- **Effects:** grain 0.04–0.06; chromatic aberration ≤1.5px on 2–3 hits only; no permanent glow.

## Sound
- Music only.
- SFX hits on 30–50% of downbeats (ticks, whooshes, low hits), each within ±1 frame of its visual event.
- The final hit lands on the logo frame. −14 LUFS.

## Banned
- The default reel (grid of colored squares, purple gradient, closing logo) with no concept.
- Random particle bursts; neon glows everywhere.
- Unrelated tableaux in a row; motion off the beat.
- More than 2 fonts or 2 accents.
- Static frames longer than 1 beat during the escalation.
- Fading to black instead of ending on a loopable hold.

## Self-check before finishing
- [ ] Concept and motif are written down, and every scene uses the motif.
- [ ] Every cut is within ±1 frame of a beat marker.
- [ ] Duration is exactly 15.0s (or the requested length); the loop seam is invisible.
- [ ] Every word is readable (≥72px, contrast ≥4.5:1).
- [ ] Ran the self-review skill, checking the contact sheet for sameness and dead beats.
