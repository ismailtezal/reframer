# Social ads and testimonials

An ad gets one second to stop the thumb and is usually watched muted. In 6–30 seconds it has to say four things: who it's for, what it does, why to believe it, and what to do next.

## Formats
| Format | Size | Used on |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Reels, TikTok, Stories |
| 4:5 | 1080×1350 | Feeds |
| 1:1 | 1080×1080 | Feeds |
| 16:9 | 1920×1080 | YouTube, X |

Lengths: a 6s bumper, 15s or 30s. Build the master in 9:16 and adapt it.

## Timeline: 15s
| Time | Beat | Build |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1s | Thumb-stop | A bold 3–6 word claim (96–120px, slamming or popping in by word, stagger 2f) over the product or a face, or a before/after split. Motion on frame 0. |
| 1–4s | Problem | One line naming the pain, over footage of the "before" state. |
| 4–10s | Product | 2 benefits of ~3s each: real footage or UI with an arrow-callout or spec-card; an emoji-pop at most once. |
| 10–13s | Proof | 5 stars filling (stagger 3f each, smooth) with a quote-card of a real review, or a counter (customers, rating). |
| 13–15s | CTA | The offer and the action ("Shop now", URL) on an end-card with the logo, held ≥2s. |

- **6s bumper:** hook and product (0–2s), one benefit (2–4.5s), logo and CTA (4.5–6s).
- **30s:** add a second benefit block and a second testimonial.

## Testimonials and reviews
- Only real reviews, from the user or a public source. Keep the wording verbatim (trim with "…" only). Attribute them: first name and last initial, platform, and date when known.
- **Quote-card:**
  - big quote marks, 120–160px, in the accent at 20% opacity;
  - the quote at 48–56px, ≤25 words;
  - one review every 3–4s.
- **Rating:** a real number from a real platform; stars fill left to right.
- **UGC style:** follow the footage-polish rules, keep captions on, and show the creator's face in the first second.

## Before / after
- A split-screen with a wipe divider moving 0→50% over 18f (smooth); labels at 40–48px.
- Use the same framing, lighting and timing on both sides so the comparison is honest.

## Layout
- Burn in captions for any voice (shorts-captions sizes).
- 9:16 uses the shorts safe zones. In 4:5, keep key text inside the central 1080×1080.
- The logo appears by 3s (in a corner, ≤12% of the width) or as the final lock.
- Brand colors, ≤2 fonts, and one accent for the CTA and highlights.

## Variants
Make 2–3 hook variants (different first 2s) on the same body for A/B testing, and name them clearly.

## Sound
- Licensed upbeat music, 110–130 BPM, with cuts on the beats.
- SFX only on the hook, the star fill and the CTA.
- −14 LUFS. A voiceover is optional and always captioned.

## Compliance
- No fake reviews, testimonials or scarcity. A countdown only for a real deadline.
- No misleading before/after shots.
- Every claim must be backed up.
- Label sponsored creator content as the platform requires.

## Banned
- A logo intro before the hook.
- Text-heavy frames (more than 12 words).
- Tiny prices or terms (under 32px).
- Stock montages of smiling people.
- Fake UI or results; emoji spam.
- More than one CTA; ending without the action and URL.

## Self-check before finishing
- [ ] The hook is on frame 0, with the product or a face visible by 1s.
- [ ] The message works muted, with captions burned in.
- [ ] Every review, rating and number is real and attributed.
- [ ] The CTA is held ≥2s, and the safe zones are respected for each format.
- [ ] Ran the self-review skill.
