# Motion taste: premium vs amateur

Left alone, agents produce the median: a centered headline on a purple-to-blue gradient that fades up, which viewers read instantly as AI slop. The videos people loved have:
- the real subject on screen;
- cuts with rhythm and motion that lands on the music;
- one accent color;
- confident type with strong scale contrast.

Polish alone doesn't make a video go viral, but sloppy work kills it, so meet this bar every time. Frames assume 30fps (frames = seconds × fps).

## Decide before building
- Name a concept and a reference style (Apple keynote, Swiss grid, Vox explainer, 3Blue1Brown, Wes Anderson), not adjectives like "modern and sleek".
- Write a shot list with seconds, one idea per shot.
- Use the brand kit and real assets: logo, screenshots, recordings, data. Draw a gradient only when there is truly nothing to show.

## Legibility minimums (1080 short side)
| Role | 16:9 | 9:16 |
|---|---|---|
| Hero / headline | 96–160px (floor 72) | 96–140px (floor 80) |
| Subhead | 48–64px | 56–72px |
| Body | ≥40px, ≥48px if watched in a phone feed | ≥48px |
| Labels, UI callouts | ≥32px | ≥36px |
| Footnotes, sources | floor 28px | floor 32px |

- Hold text for at least 0.3s per word + 0.6s (minimum 1.2s). ≤7 words per headline line, ≤12 words on screen at once.
- Contrast ≥4.5:1 for body, ≥3:1 for hero type. Put a 30–50% black scrim behind text on busy footage.
- Display tracking −0.02 to −0.04em, line height 0.95–1.1; uppercase labels +0.06 to +0.12em. ≤2 font families; changing numbers use tabular or mono digits.
- 16:9: keep text ≥96px from the sides and ≥64px from the top and bottom. 9:16: see the shorts-captions skill.

## Pacing
- First motion on frame 0–3. Never open on a static or black second.
- Promos and shorts: something new every 2–4s, but vary it (many short shots, a few long ones; never 5 shots of equal length in a row) and keep at least one 1.5s+ hold per 30s after a key line or reveal. Deliberate holds up to 6s are fine.
- Average shot length: hype 0.5–1.5s, launch 1.5–3s, explainer and keynote 3–6s.
- Put the motion in the first 10–15 frames of a shot, then settle (≤2% drift); text that keeps moving shimmers.
- Hard cut by default: ≥90% of edit points are plain cuts. Transitions only for meaning (push = next step, zoom-in = deeper, dip-to-black = time or chapter change, whip = energy, light-leak at most once per film); see the transitions skill.

## Motion and easing
- Entrances 12–18f, hero reveals 20–30f, exits 6–10f (or just cut).
- Staggers: chars 1–2f, words 2–4f, lines 4–8f, cards 3–5f; a whole sequence ≤0.8s.
- Easing:
  - smooth for most entrances;
  - snappy for UI cards and buttons (overshoot ≤3%);
  - heavy for big type and impacts;
  - apple for keynote rise-blur;
  - playful and bouncy only for mascots, emoji and meme content.
  - Never linear on spatial moves (drifts, ken-burns and tickers excepted).
- Prefer rise-blur, mask-reveal or wipe over a plain fade. Pair one huge word (300px+, bleeding off frame) with small supporting text.
- Depth: parallax, tilt ≤12°, soft drop-shadows (opacity 0.2–0.35).
- Effects: grain 0.03–0.06, vignette 0.15–0.3, chromatic aberration ≤1.5px on hits only, shake only on impacts (≤6px, ≤6f), glow only on real light sources.

## Color
Neutral base plus one accent (60/30/10). Dark: #0B0B0F–#141318 with #EDEDED text. Light: #F5F5F2–#E6E8E6 with #111111 text. The brand kit overrides both. Keep gradients subtle and on-brand, never the default purple-to-blue.

## Sound
- Most of the best-liked launch films and reels are music-only. Burn in captions whenever someone speaks.
- Cut on beats and land the hero moment on a downbeat or the drop, using the beat markers from the music analysis.
- SFX sparse (≤6–8 per minute for polished and short-form, ≤12 for explainers), each on a visible event within ±1 frame; see the sound-design skill.
- −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP; duck music 12–18 dB under voice. End on a musical resolution, never mid-bar.

## Banned
- The centered gradient headline; fade as the only animation; everything entering at once.
- Particle bursts, lens flares, random shake, neon glow on UI, bouncy UI chrome.
- Crossfades on every cut; title-plus-bullets slideshows; idle wiggles on everything.
- Invented UI, metrics, logos or reviews; lorem ipsum.
- Text below the minimums or outside safe zones; more than 2 fonts or 1 accent.
- Music cut mid-phrase.

## Self-check before finishing
- [ ] Frame 0 moves; the subject is on screen by 3s.
- [ ] All text passes the size, contrast, reading-time and safe-zone rules.
- [ ] One accent, ≤2 fonts, real assets only.
- [ ] Cuts land on beats; −14 LUFS; a clean ending.
- [ ] Human-edited clips untouched; choices explained in clip notes.
- [ ] Ran the self-review skill.
