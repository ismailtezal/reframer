# Apple-keynote style

Viewers praise this look as having no AI slop. Its parts:
- Restraint, with one idea per shot.
- Huge confident type and the product as the hero.
- Generous negative space.
- Eased, overlapping motion and a camera that never jerks.

It should feel controlled, sparse and tactile, never like a template or a slideshow.

## Format
1920×1080, 30fps, 20–45s. Music: minimal piano, strings or restrained electronic, 80–110 BPM. There is an optional single-continuous-take variant with no cuts, where the camera travels across one large canvas.

## Palette and type
- Background pure black #000000 or soft white #F5F5F7. Text #F5F5F7 on dark, #1D1D1F on light; secondary text at 60% opacity.
- At most one accent, used as a 2-stop gradient fill on a single hero word.
- One sans family (Inter, Manrope or the brand font). Headlines: weight 600–700, 120–200px, tracking −0.03em, line height 1.0. Supporting line: 40–48px, weight 500.
- ≤6 words per headline. Centering is fine for a single hero word on a flat background; everything else sits on a deliberate grid.

## Timeline (30s)
| Time | Beat | Build |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Statement | One word or a short line. Text animation rise-blur by word, 24–30f per word, stagger 4f, apple easing. |
| 3–10s | Product hero | Product image or device-frame with a slow push: scale 1.00→1.06 across the shot, gentle easing. One light-sweep across the product at about 5s. Soft drop-shadow (radius 60, y 30, opacity 0.25). |
| 10–24s | 3 features, ~4.5s each | A headline (rise-blur, 24f, apple) and one supporting line (rise, 18f, starting 8f later). A close-up crop of the product moving slowly in the same direction. Numbers ("2× faster", "18 hours") via a counter or spec-card, settling with smooth easing over 24–36f. |
| 24–30s | Payoff | One closing line, then a logo-reveal on black or white, held ≥2.5s, ending on the final chord or on silence. |

## Motion rules
- Entrances 20–30f with apple or smooth easing. Exits 10–12f blur or fade, or a clean cut.
- Overlap: each new element starts 6–10f before the previous one fully settles. Nothing pops, bounces, spins or glitches.
- Camera: scale changes ≤6%, rotation ≤8°, one direction per shot, no snap zooms.
- Hold every landed line in stillness for ≥1.5s.
- Transitions: a hard cut, or a slow fade or blur (12–18f) between chapters; dip-to-white at most once.
- **Continuous-take variant:** nothing cuts, fades or blurs between ideas. Objects transform into the next idea (matching position, color or shape) while the camera glides 30–45f between them with smooth easing.

## Finish
- Grade: contrast 1.05, saturation 0.95–1.0, vignette 0.15–0.2.
- Grain 0–0.03.
- Glow only on the product's own highlights.

## Sound
- 3–6 SFX in the whole film: a soft whoosh, a shimmer, and a single low hit on the logo.
- Let silence work: leave a beat of quiet before the reveal.
- −14 LUFS; let the last chord ring out naturally.

## Banned
- Particles, lens flares, camera shake, glitch.
- Pop, bounce or elastic easing; emoji.
- Busy grid or neon backgrounds; stock footage; more than one accent.
- Whip transitions.
- Spec dumps (more than 3 specs on screen); text under 40px.
- More than one idea per shot; rushed shots under 2s.

## Self-check before finishing
- [ ] Every shot has one idea and ≥1.5s of stillness after landing.
- [ ] One accent, one type family, strong headline scale contrast.
- [ ] No easing with visible overshoot anywhere.
- [ ] Camera moves are slow, continuous and one-directional per shot.
- [ ] Logo held ≥2.5s on the final chord.
- [ ] Ran the self-review skill.
