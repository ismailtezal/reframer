# Launch video playbook

Launch films and product demos are the most-shared AI-made videos. The best-liked ones ran about 29s, mostly 16:9 and music-only, with the real product on screen fast. Two patterns stand out among the favorites:
- They rebuilt the actual interface (terminal install, chat panes, model picker, settings) instead of drawing abstract shapes.
- The motion was springy and staggered, on a near-black background with one warm accent.

## Gather first (ask at most 3 questions if anything is missing)
- Product name and a one-line promise.
- Logo plus brand colors and fonts.
- Real screenshots or screen recordings (or a URL to capture).
- The 3 features that matter.
- One proof point (a real number, logo or review).
- CTA and URL.
- A music track.

Never invent UI copy, labels, numbers or customers. Use the product's own words.

## Format
1920×1080, 30fps, 25–35s. Cutdowns: 15s, and 9:16 via the shorts-captions skill. Music-only by default; if there is a voiceover, burn in captions.

## Timeline (30s)
| Time | Beat | Build |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.5s | Hook | A 2–5 word problem or promise as a kinetic-title: 120–160px, left-aligned, mask-up by word, stagger 3f, smooth. Motion starts on frame 0. |
| 2.5–6s | Reveal | A logo-reveal that hands off to the product: a browser-window or device-frame with the real UI. It enters with rise (18f, snappy), tilted 8–10° on X, drifting from 10° to −10° on Y across the shot, scale 0.94→1.0. The product must be visible by 3s. |
| 6–20s | 3 features, ~4.5s each | A 2–4 word headline (88–110px, left third) next to the real UI. A cursor-click component on the action, punching in 1.0→1.5 toward the click (18–24f, smooth), then holding. One callout per feature: arrow-callout, circle-highlight or spec-card. Hard cut between features, on a downbeat. |
| 20–25s | Proof | A stat-grid or counter with real numbers (30–45f, smooth), or a quote-card with a real customer quote. Show logos only if they are real. |
| 25–30s | CTA | Logo + URL (≥56px) + end-card, held ≥2s. The final music hit lands on the logo lock. |

## Build details
- **Terminal installs:** a terminal-window component. Type the command by character (stagger 1f) and stagger the output lines every 1.5–2f. The window jumps in on a fast spring with no bounce.
- **UI panels and cards:** in-animation rise, or pop at intensity 0.5, with snappy easing; stagger children 3–4f; overshoot ≤3%.
- **Camera:** one purposeful move per shot (punch-in, push or slow drift). Keep the motion in the first 10–15 frames, then hold still while text is read.
- **Background:** the brand background, or near-black (#0C0A09–#111114) with a subtle grid-background (lines ≤8% opacity) or a desaturated gradient-mesh. Use one accent (e.g. amber #FBBF24 on stone black) for highlights, the cursor and the CTA.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts. At most one whip or push between sections, and a light-leak into the CTA only if it fits.
- **Changelog or feature-drop variant (15–20s):** a chapter-card ("New in <Product>"), then 1–3 features, then an end-card ("Available today").

## Sound
- Music at 100–128 BPM.
- Use the beat markers: cut features on downbeats and put the drop on the reveal or the hero feature.
- SFX: soft clicks on cursor clicks and one whoosh on a whip, nothing else.
- −14 LUFS, ending on the downbeat that locks the logo.

## Cutdowns
- **15s:** hook 2s → reveal 3s → 2 features × 3s → CTA 4s.
- **9:16:** device-frame in the middle third, headline above it (y 300–600), CTA above the bottom 420px.

## Banned
- Invented UI, fake metrics, fake logos or testimonials, lorem ipsum.
- More than 3 features.
- A gradient where the product should be; all-centered layouts.
- Feature text under 40px.
- Crossfades between every shot; bouncy UI.
- Particles, lens flares, rocket emoji.
- Ending without a URL.

## Self-check before finishing
- [ ] Product on screen by 3.0s; first motion on frame 0.
- [ ] Every UI string and number is real and readable at 720p.
- [ ] One message per feature shot; each shot ≤5s.
- [ ] Cuts on downbeats; logo lock on a hit; −14 LUFS.
- [ ] CTA and URL held ≥2s.
- [ ] Ran the self-review skill.
