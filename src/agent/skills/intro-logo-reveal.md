# Intros, titles and logo reveals

Keep these short and confident, synced to a sound hit. The tech intros people loved used dark grades, a single light sweep or leak, kinetic type and one impact frame. Long intros lose viewers.

## Durations
| Piece | Length |
|---|---|
| Logo sting | 2–4s |
| YouTube/channel intro | 3–5s, never more than 6s; better still, a cold-open hook first and the intro after it |
| Cinematic title sequence | 6–12s |
| Outro / end card | 5–20s |

## Logo rules
- Use the real logo asset from the brand kit (a transparent SVG or PNG).
- Never redraw it, recolor it off-brand, stretch it, or leave it rotated.
- Keep clear space of at least half the logo's height around it.
- For stings, the logo fills 20–35% of the frame width.
- Use the light or dark version to suit the background.

## Timeline: 4s logo reveal
| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0–0.5s | Anticipation: a dark frame (#050507–#0B0B0F) with a slow light-sweep or dim glow-orbs (≤20% opacity). Motion starts on frame 0 and a whoosh rises. |
| 0.5–2.0s | Build: the mark enters with mask-reveal or wipe (18–24f, smooth). The wordmark rises by character with rise-blur (stagger 1–2f, heavy or smooth), or its outlines draw on. |
| 2.0s | Lock frame: everything settles (snappy, scale 1.03→1.00 in 8f) with one accent pulse: a flash transition, a light-leak, or glow at 0.3–0.4 for about 10f. The impact SFX lands on this exact frame (±1f). |
| 2.0–4.0s | Hold with a breathe loop of ≤1.5%. The tagline (40–56px, 60–70% opacity) rises in at about 2.4s over 12–15f. |

## Cinematic tech intro (8s)
- **Frame:** optional 2.39:1 letterbox.
- **Background:** a gradient-mesh or grid-background (lines ≤10% opacity), with glow-orbs drifting slowly.
- **Title:** a kinetic-title that scrambles or masks up, 120–180px, tracking +0.02 to +0.08em for uppercase titles.
- **Grade:**
  - contrast 1.1;
  - slightly lifted blacks (fade 0.05);
  - temperature ±0.1 (cool or warm);
  - vignette 0.25, grain 0.04.
- **Hits:** one zoom-blur or whip into the title, with chromatic aberration ≤1.5px on the hit only.
- **Structure:**
  - 0–2s: atmosphere;
  - 2–5s: title build;
  - 5–6s: impact;
  - 6–8s: hold, then exit with a 10f dip-to-black or a cut on the beat.

## YouTube outro / end card
- An end-card component of 10–20s.
- Leave room for 1–2 video tiles and a subscribe element, inside the central 90% of the frame.
- Background motion stays slow (drift), and the music resolves at the end.

## Retro and texture variants
- film-burn and vhs-overlay only when the brief is retro or analog.
- paper-texture for handmade brands.
- Never stack more than two textures.

## Sound
- A riser or whoosh (0–0.5s), then the impact on the lock frame, then a shimmer or tail.
- The music sting resolves within 1s of the lock.
- −14 LUFS; intros can run hot but must never clip.

## Banned
- Spinning chrome 3D logos without a brief that asks for them.
- Lens flares; glitch, shake and RGB split all at once.
- Channel intros over 5s.
- Distorted or redrawn logos.
- Taglines too fast to read (under 1.5s on screen).
- More than one flash; stacking 3 or more effects.
- An impact not synced to a visual event.

## Self-check before finishing
- [ ] The real logo asset, in the right version, with its clear space respected.
- [ ] The lock frame and impact SFX are within ±1 frame of each other.
- [ ] The duration is in range and the tagline is readable for ≥1.5s.
- [ ] At most 2 effects, each used once.
- [ ] Ran the self-review skill.
