# App Store preview and app promo

These are two different deliverables, so decide which one first:
1. **Store preview** (App Store or Play listing): only real in-app screen captures, strict specs, and it autoplays muted.
2. **App promo** (social, website, launch): device frames, 3D tilt, music and a CTA. Follow the launch-video skill as well.

## Store preview specs (check App Store Connect before exporting)
- 15–30s, ≤30fps, H.264. Up to 3 previews per device size and language.
- Common sizes:
  - 886×1920 portrait / 1920×886 landscape for 6.5"–6.9" iPhones;
  - 1080×1920 for the 5.5" iPhone;
  - 1200×1600 for the 12.9"/13" iPad.
- Apple allows only screen captures of the app itself, plus text overlays and narration. No hands, people or devices, and no footage that isn't the app. Avoid prices, rankings and time-limited claims.
- Google Play uses a YouTube link. Make the first 30s stand on their own, landscape-friendly unless the app is portrait-only.
- Pick a strong poster frame: core UI plus a headline.

## Capture
- Record at native resolution, at 60fps if possible (export at 30).
- A clean status bar: 9:41, full battery and signal, no notifications.
- Realistic demo data: no lorem ipsum and no real personal data.
- One appearance (light or dark) throughout.
- Record each feature as its own 4–8s take.

## Timeline: 25s store preview
| Time | Beat | Build |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Core value | The app's main moment, already in motion, with a 2–5 word headline at the top (64–80px on a 1080 width, within y 120–360). |
| 3–20s | 3–4 features, 4–5s each | A full-bleed capture with a new headline for each feature (rise 12–15f, snappy, words staggered 2–3f). A touch-style cursor-click on every tap (a 40–56px ripple, 20–24f). Where the UI is small, punch in 1.0→1.3 toward the detail (18f, smooth). |
| 20–25s | Payoff | The app's best end state and its name, held ≥2s. |

- Between features, push in the direction the UI navigates (10–12f), or hard cut. Never spin or flip.
- It must work muted: the on-screen headline carries every point. Music is optional; keep tap SFX subtle.

## App promo variant (9:16 or 16:9, 15–30s)
- A device-frame with the capture inside, tilted 8–12° and drifting slowly. The background is a gradient-mesh or glow-orbs in brand colors, kept subtle.
- Structure:
  - a hook headline at 0–1.5s;
  - 3 features;
  - ratings only if real (e.g. 4.8 ★ copied from the live listing);
  - an end-card with the store badge and a CTA.
- 9:16 uses the safe zones from the shorts-captions skill. A 16:9 hero can show 2–3 devices staggered 4–6f.

## Type
- Headlines 64–88px (on a 1080 width), weight 700–800, ≤5 words, one line where possible.
- All other text ≥48px.

## Banned
- Fake UI or unreleased features.
- Hands, people or devices in store previews.
- Prices, "#1", awards or rankings without proof, and never in store previews.
- Notifications or messy status bars; personal data.
- Tiny unreadable UI without a punch-in; headlines covering important UI.
- Letterboxed landscape footage in a portrait slot.

## Self-check before finishing
- [ ] The right resolution, fps and a 15–30s duration for the target slot.
- [ ] Every frame is a real app capture (store preview).
- [ ] Each feature reads muted, with a headline of ≤5 words.
- [ ] A clean status bar and demo data only.
- [ ] A poster frame is chosen; ran the self-review skill.
